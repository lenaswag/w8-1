Idioms: More Than Just Words

Have you ever heard someone say, "It's a piece of cake"? The person is not talking about food. This idiom means that something is very easy.

Idioms are expressions whose meanings are different from the meanings of their individual words. They make everyday conversations more interesting. Many English idioms have been used for hundreds of years.

Some idioms come from interesting stories. For example, "break the ice" means to make people feel more comfortable in a new situation. This expression has been used in many social situations.

Today, English idioms have been shared through movies, books, and social media. Learning idioms can help us understand English speakers and their cultures better.

주요 표현
  • a piece of cake — 아주 쉬운 일
  • break the ice — 어색한 분위기를 깨다
  • individual — 개별적인
  • expression — 표현
  • social situation — 사회적 상황
